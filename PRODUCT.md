# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal AirDunia operations teams (Super Admin: founders and ops managers), corporate travel managers (Biz Admin), and travel agency senior agents (Partner Admin). Used at a desk, on a monitor, in focused work sessions. Users are task-oriented and data-literate — they manage bookings, review approvals, set commissions, and control permissions.

## Product Purpose

Three-portal admin system for AirDunia travel platform. Super Admin controls the full platform. Biz Admin manages corporate travel for organizations. Partner Admin manages agent hierarchies and commissions. Success means users complete their primary task in the fewest clicks with complete confidence in the data shown.

## Brand Personality

Modern, confident, precise. Stripe-meets-Linear energy. Feels like a tool built by people who use it.

## Anti-references

- Bloated SaaS dashboards with gradient hero metrics on every screen
- Clunky bootstrap-era admin panels
- Over-decorated cards with icon tiles above every heading
- Pure-black dark themes that feel heavy and dated

## Design Principles

1. **Earned familiarity** — Use standard affordances. The interface disappears into the task.
2. **Hierarchy over decoration** — Strong type weight contrast and spacing do the visual work; not color, not gradients.
3. **Data first** — Tables, numbers, and states are the product. They must be legible at density.
4. **Confident restraint** — One accent color, used precisely for primary actions and active states only.
5. **State completeness** — Every interactive element has hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, and error states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast. All interactive elements keyboard-navigable.
